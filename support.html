Help & Support

Answers to common questions, or get in touch.

Contact us

Write to support@physique.app and we’ll get back to you, usually within two working days. Please include your app version and device if you’re reporting a problem.

Frequently asked questions

How do I create an account or sign in?

You can sign up with Sign in with Apple, Google or your email address. Use the same method each time so you reach the same PHYSIQUE account.

What does PHYSIQUE Pro cost?

PHYSIQUE Pro is an auto-renewing subscription, billed monthly or yearly through your App Store account. In Denmark it costs 89 DKK per month or 899 DKK per year; prices in other countries are shown in the app and the App Store before you buy. There is no free trial. The free plan stays usable without subscribing.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the App Store or Google Play. Go to Settings → Subscription → Manage subscription, or open your store's subscription settings. You keep Pro until the end of the paid period. On iPhone you can also go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → PHYSIQUE. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel a subscription.

I paid but don't have Pro. What do I do?

Make sure you're signed in to the same store account, then go to Settings → Subscription → Restore purchases. If it still doesn't work, email us with roughly when you purchased.

Can I get a copy of my data?

Yes — Settings → Export your data creates a file with everything we store, plus links to your photos.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete account. Everything is erased permanently. Remember to cancel any store subscription first, because deleting the account doesn't cancel it.

Can I delete my photos?

Yes. Delete any progress photo from the Progress tab. Meal photos are deleted automatically after 30 days; the meal itself stays in your diary.

How accurate are AI food estimates?

They're estimates. The AI recognises foods and guesses portions; nutrition values then come from food databases. Portion size is the biggest source of error, so check the amounts before saving — your corrections also help future suggestions.

How are my calorie and macro targets calculated?

From your age, height, weight, activity, training days and goal using established formulas, with safety floors. Adaptive adjustments only happen after enough weigh-ins and logged days, change by small steps, and always ask you before applying.

What happens if I train without internet?

Every set is saved on your phone first and syncs automatically when you're back online. If the same workout was changed on another device, we ask you which version to keep.

Why am I not getting notifications?

Check that notifications are allowed for PHYSIQUE in your phone's settings, that the category is on under Settings → Notifications, and that the time isn't inside your quiet hours.

How do I change units or language?

Go to Settings → Units & language. Changes apply straight away, and your data is converted automatically.

Refunds

Payments are handled by Apple, so refunds for App Store purchases are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Health Disclaimer

PHYSIQUE is a fitness and nutrition tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease or medical condition, and it does not replace advice from a doctor, dietitian or physiotherapist.

Everything is an estimate. Calorie and macro targets, AI food recognition, portion sizes, body-fat estimates and progress interpretations are approximate and can be wrong. Check them, adjust them, and use your own judgement.

Talk to a doctor first before you start a new training or nutrition plan if you:

  • are pregnant or breastfeeding,
  • have or have had an eating disorder,
  • have a medical condition (for example heart, kidney or metabolic disease, diabetes or high blood pressure) or take medication that affects weight or appetite,
  • have an injury, pain or a physical limitation,
  • are under 18.

Stop and seek medical help if you feel chest pain, dizziness, shortness of breath, faintness, or unusual pain during or after exercise. In an emergency, call 112.

Your wellbeing comes first. PHYSIQUE encourages sustainable habits, not extreme dieting. If food, weight or exercise is causing you distress, please reach out to your doctor or a support organisation such as LMS – Landsforeningen mod spiseforstyrrelser og selvskade Contact details: to be confirmed.

# How PHYSIQUE uses AI

**You are talking to an AI, not a person.** The PHYSIQUE coach and food scanner are powered by AI models from **Anthropic** (Claude). No human reads your messages or photos to answer you.

### What the AI does
- **Food photos:** suggests which foods are on your plate, how much, and how they were cooked. Calories and macros are then looked up in a nutrition database – not invented by the AI. **You review and confirm everything before it is saved.**
- **Coach:** answers your questions and explains your progress using a summary of your data (goal, training, average intake, weight and measurement trends).
- **Reports and plans:** writes your weekly report and drafts workouts or programs you can edit.

### What the AI doesn't do
- It **doesn't decide your calorie targets** – those come from a standard formula you can see and change.
- It **doesn't change your plan or delete anything** without your confirmation.
- It **doesn't give medical advice** or diagnoses, and it can't see your progress photos.
- It **isn't always right.** Portions, foods and advice can be wrong – check them.

### Your data
- Only the data needed for your request is sent, through our servers. Your name and email are not sent.
- **Anthropic may not use your data to train its models**, and deletes it from its systems within 30 days (longer only if required by law or to investigate misuse).
- Meal photos are deleted from PHYSIQUE after 30 days.
- **Your progress photos are never used to train AI** unless you separately opt in.

### Your choice
AI features need your permission. You can say no or switch them off any time in **Settings → Privacy → AI**. You can still log food manually or by barcode, and track your training, without AI.

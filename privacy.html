PHYSIQUE – Privacy Policy

Version: 1.0
Effective date: To be confirmed
Language: English. A Danish version is available.

1. Summary

  • PHYSIQUE helps you track training, food, bodyweight, body measurements and progress photos, and gives you AI-assisted food recognition and coaching.
  • Some of what you log is health data, which gets extra protection under the GDPR. We only process it with your explicit consent, and you can withdraw that consent at any time.
  • AI features send the content you choose (for example a meal photo, a text description or a coaching question, together with relevant parts of your training and nutrition data) to our AI provider, Anthropic, to produce a result. Anthropic may not use this data to train its models under its commercial terms.
  • Progress photos are private. They are never public, never shared with other users, and never used to train any AI model unless you give a separate, explicit, opt-in consent. You can delete them at any time.
  • Meal photos are deleted automatically 30 days after upload (we may change this period, and will update this policy if we do). The meal entry (foods, quantities, calories, macros) stays in your diary.
  • We do not sell your data, show third-party advertising, or track you across other companies' apps and websites.
  • You can export or delete your data in the app at any time.

4. The data we process

We only ask for what we need. Fields marked "optional" are never required to use the app.

4.1 Account data

  • Email address, password (stored only as a secure hash by our hosting provider), or the identifier you get from Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, we receive a relay address.
  • Name or display name (if provided), optional profile photo.
  • Account creation date, email verification status, login timestamps.

4.2 Profile and preferences

  • Date of birth (used to calculate age for estimates and to apply age safeguards).
  • Sex or physiological information where it changes an estimate (e.g. basal metabolic rate). You can choose "prefer not to say", in which case a sex-neutral estimate is used.
  • Height, country, preferred units, language, time zone.
  • Fitness experience, training frequency, goals, training preferences, available equipment, favourite and avoided exercises.
  • Dietary pattern (e.g. vegetarian, vegan), allergies and food exclusions.

4.3 Health-related data (special category data, GDPR Art. 9)

We treat the following as data concerning health:

  • Bodyweight, body measurements (waist, hips, chest, arms, thighs, calves, neck, custom), estimated body-fat percentage, and target weight.
  • Progress photos (front, side, back, custom).
  • Optional safety answers: whether you are pregnant or breastfeeding, whether you have a history of an eating disorder, whether you have a medical condition, and any free-text notes you add about medical conditions or injuries/physical limitations.
  • Recovery check-ins (e.g. sleep quality, soreness, fatigue).
  • Food and nutrition logs (meals, calories, macronutrients) and training logs, to the extent they reveal information about your health.
  • Allergies and dietary restrictions, to the extent they reveal health information.

Pregnancy and medical-condition answers are only used to keep recommendations safe (for example, to avoid generating a calorie deficit). They are optional.

4.4 Food logging data

  • Meal photos you take or select for AI analysis.
  • Text or voice descriptions of meals. If you use voice input, speech is converted to text by your phone's operating system speech-recognition service (Apple or Google), which may process audio on the device or on Apple's/Google's servers under their terms. We receive only the resulting text, not the audio.
  • Barcodes you scan, foods, quantities, recipes, saved meals and your corrections to AI estimates. Your corrections are stored as your personal data and are used to make your future logging faster and more accurate (for example, remembering your usual portion sizes). They are not used to train AI models.

4.5 Training data

Workouts, exercises, sets, repetitions, weights, personal records, programs, and notes.

4.6 AI coach data

Messages you send to the AI coach, the coach's responses, summaries of earlier conversations, weekly reports, recommendations, and whether you accepted or rejected them.

4.7 Subscription data

Subscription status, product, start/renewal/expiry dates, trial status, billing issues, and an anonymous app user ID. Payments are handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never receive your card details or full payment information.

4.8 Device and technical data

  • Installation ID, platform (iOS/Android), app version, operating system version, language/locale, time zone.
  • Push notification token (if you allow notifications).
  • IP address and request logs, processed by our hosting provider for security and operation.
  • Error and crash reports (error message, technical context such as the screen and network state; never your meal photos, measurements or message content).

4.9 Usage analytics (only with consent)

If you allow it, we record in-app events such as "onboarding completed", "first meal logged", "paywall viewed" or "weekly report opened", together with a random installation ID, app version and platform. We use our own analytics stored with our hosting provider – no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

4.10 Communication with us

Emails and other messages you send to support, and our replies.

6. How the AI features work and what is shared

PHYSIQUE uses large language models provided by Anthropic PBC (USA) to:

  • Recognise food in meal photos and estimate portion sizes and preparation. The AI suggests foods and quantities; the nutritional values are then looked up in a nutrition database, and you confirm or edit everything before it is saved.
  • Coach you: answer your questions, explain trends and write weekly reports. To do this, our servers send the AI a compact summary of the relevant data – for example your goal, recent training performance, average calorie and protein intake, weight trend and measurement changes – along with your message.
  • Draft workouts and programs that you can review and edit.

What we do to protect you:

  • All AI requests go through our own servers. The app never contacts the AI provider directly.
  • We send only the data needed for the task you asked for. We do not send your name or email address to the AI provider.
  • Progress photos are not sent to the AI provider.
  • Under Anthropic's Commercial Terms of Service, Anthropic may not train its models on our customers' content submitted through the API (verified against Anthropic's Commercial Terms, version effective 17 June 2025).
  • According to Anthropic's privacy documentation, API inputs and outputs are deleted from Anthropic's systems within 30 days, except where a longer period is required by law or where content is flagged for violating Anthropic's Usage Policy (in which case it may be retained for up to 2 years).
  • AI output can be wrong. Food recognition and portion estimates are approximate. See our AI disclosure and health disclaimer.

7. Photos

7.1 Progress photos

  • Stored in a private, encrypted storage bucket. They can only be accessed by you through short-lived, signed links.
  • Never public, never visible to other users, never used in marketing.
  • Never used to train AI models unless you give a separate, explicit, opt-in consent, which you can withdraw at any time. At launch we do not ask for this consent and do not train on photos.
  • Our staff do not view your progress photos. Access is technically restricted; in exceptional cases (e.g. you ask support to investigate a problem with a specific photo) access requires your request and is logged.
  • Kept until you delete them or delete your account.

7.2 Meal photos

  • Uploaded to a private bucket, sent to our AI provider for analysis, and automatically deleted 30 days after upload. We may change this period (it is a configurable setting); if we extend it, we will update this policy and inform you in advance.
  • The resulting diary entry (food names, quantities, nutrition values) is kept as part of your food log.
  • You can delete a meal photo earlier by deleting the meal entry.

7.3 Profile photo

Stored privately and kept until you remove it or delete your account.

11. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you.
  • Data portability – get your data in a structured, machine-readable format. Use Settings → Privacy → Export my data.
  • Rectification – correct inaccurate data (most data can be edited directly in the app).
  • Erasure – delete individual entries, photos, or your whole account.
  • Restriction of processing in certain cases.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests (section 5), including at any time to direct marketing.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, in Settings → Privacy (health data, AI processing, analytics, marketing, photo training). Withdrawal does not affect processing that already took place. If you withdraw health-data consent, we will stop processing that data and the related features will stop working; we will ask you whether you also want the data deleted.
  • Not be subject to solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects (we do not make such decisions).

To exercise your rights, use the in-app settings or email support@physique.app. We respond within one month (extendable by two months for complex requests, in which case we will tell you). We may need to verify your identity.

12. Children and age limits

PHYSIQUE is not intended for anyone under 16. During sign-up we ask for your date of birth, and we do not allow accounts for users under 16.

Because nutrition and weight-change features can affect young people's health, the app applies extra safeguards for users who are under 18 or outside safe adult recommendations – for example, no aggressive weight-loss targets.

If we learn that we hold data about a child under 16, we will delete it. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us.

13. Security

We protect your data with, among other things:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS) and encryption at rest by our hosting provider.
  • Row-level security so each user can only access their own data.
  • Private storage buckets and short-lived signed links for photos.
  • AI provider keys and other secrets are kept on our servers, never in the app.
  • Rate limiting, input validation, verified webhook signatures, and audit logs for administrator actions.
  • Access to personal data limited to staff who need it, with separate administrator accounts.

No system is completely secure. If a personal data breach is likely to result in a high risk to you, we will notify you and the Danish Data Protection Agency as required by law.
